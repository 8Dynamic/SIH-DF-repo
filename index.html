Common questions

Short answers to what most applicants ask first.

How does Scheme Recommendation System pick schemes for me?

It compares your profile with scheme rules such as age, gender, category, sector, business stage, location, project cost, household income and education.

Is a high match a guarantee?

No. The match percentage is only a prototype recommendation score. Final eligibility is decided by the relevant department or lender.

What is the acceptance rate?

Acceptance rate is calculated as approved applications divided by applications received. In this prototype, figures marked as demo are illustrative and should be replaced with verified official scheme-level statistics.

What does money received percentage mean?

It represents applications that reached the money-disbursed stage divided by applications received. It should not be interpreted as a guarantee that an applicant will receive funds.

Can I calculate EMI?

Yes. Enter the loan amount, annual interest rate and tenure in the EMI calculator above.

Do I need to pay to apply?

Government scheme application rules vary. Use the official portal for the scheme and avoid anyone promising guaranteed approval.
